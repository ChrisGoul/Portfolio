Range on a battery quadcopter is set by the flight you can complete on one
charge. If the aircraft can land, recharge from a panel it carries, and take off
again, the constraint becomes daylight and patience instead.

Built with MIT's Photovoltaics Lab and Conservation International to monitor
deforestation in the Andes — terrain where the distances are long and there is
nowhere to plug in. I led the student team, and the work was integrating the
pieces that make an unattended recharge cycle possible: solar charging
electronics, sensors, GPS and LIDAR modules, and the autonomy to sequence them
without anyone on site.

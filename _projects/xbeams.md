Aluminium T-slot extrusion is the default skeleton for anything built in a
workshop — robots, enclosures, shelving, test rigs. It is also heavier and more
expensive than most of those jobs need.

XBeams is the same profile in plastic: light, rigid enough for the load cases
that matter at that scale, and considerably cheaper. Sold direct at
[xbeams.net](https://xbeams.net/).

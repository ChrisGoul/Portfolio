The second stage of this press is a conventional Kawai-type multi-anvil arrangement:
six tungsten carbide anvils, with edges truncated, closing on a cubic
pressure medium with pyrophyllite gaskets that extrude and seal as the assembly
compresses.

## Where it started

This project began as a way to produce synthetic marble. Marble is a metamorphic rock —
a matrix of large-grained crystals that are somewhat translucent. Commercial
synthetic marbles are made from crushed powder and rock in a plastic or clay binder, which
approximates the surface appearance but not the translucency. Reproducing the
geologic pressures and temperatures that metamorphose limestone gets you material
that matches optically.

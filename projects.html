Materials · High pressure

HPHT Cubic Press

A laboratory cubic press capable of generating multi-GPa pressures for experimental diamond synthesis — pressure vessel design, high-temperature thermal systems, custom instrumentation and experimental sample assemblies.

Synthetic marble, the earlier version

The press started as an attempt at synthetic marble. Marble is a metamorphic rock — a matrix of large-grained crystals that appear translucent. Commercial synthetics are crushed powder and rock held in a plastic or clay binder, which approximates the surface appearance but not the translucency.

Replicating the geologic pressures and temperatures that metamorphose limestone produces material that matches the real thing optically. The same pressure-vessel problem, at higher pressures, is what the diamond work needs.

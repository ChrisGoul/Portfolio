Materials · Press

Synthetic Marble

Developed a high-pressure, high-temperature press to produce 100% pure synthetic marble without binders.

How it works

Synthetic marble floors and countertops are everywhere — but there's a noticeable visual difference between the synthetics and the real thing.

Marble is a metamorphic rock: a matrix of large-grained crystals that appear somewhat translucent. Synthetic marbles are made from crushed powder, rocks and a plastic or clay binder, which approximates the surface appearance but not the translucency.

I built this high-temperature press to replicate the geologic pressures and temperatures involved in limestone metamorphosis, producing synthetic marble that aesthetically matches the real thing.

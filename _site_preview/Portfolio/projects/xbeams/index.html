XBeams T-Slot Extrusion

A business selling lightweight, rigid plastic T-slot extrusion — a cheaper alternative to aluminium framing.

ProductManufacturingxbeams.net

Aluminium T-slot extrusion is the default skeleton for anything built in a workshop — robots, enclosures, shelving, test rigs. It is also heavier and more expensive than most of those jobs need.

XBeams is the same profile in plastic: light, rigid enough for the load cases that matter at that scale, and considerably cheaper. Sold direct at xbeams.net.

Temperature Controlled Forge

A propane forge that holds a set temperature under PID control, built for the MIT Blacksmithing Lab.

MachineControlsMIT Blacksmithing Lab

A propane forge normally runs wherever the burner and the day put it, and you judge the steel by colour. That is fine for forging and poor for heat treatment, where the number matters.

This one closes the loop: measure the chamber, modulate the gas, hold a setpoint. Built for the MIT Blacksmithing Lab.

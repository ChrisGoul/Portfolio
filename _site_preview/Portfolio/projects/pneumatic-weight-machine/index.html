Pneumatic Weight Machine

A portable home gym with constant concentric and eccentric loading to 150 kg, using pneumatic actuators and closed-loop cable tension control.

Three versions150 kgClosed-loop tension control

Stacked plates give you one thing a pneumatic cylinder does not: constant force. Air compresses, so as a piston travels its pressure rises and the load the user feels climbs through the rep. Each version of this machine is a different answer to that problem.

Three attempts at constant force

V1 — a large air tank. Make the reservoir big enough relative to the swept volume and the pressure rise over a stroke becomes small. It works, and it is bulky.

V2 — a mechanical fix instead. A sprocket with a changing radius winds the cable, so as pressure rises the moment arm shrinks and the cable tension stays flat. Proof of concept, less polished.

V3 — closed-loop control. Measure cable tension, regulate pressure against it, and the profile becomes something you set rather than something the geometry gives you. Compact, portable, adjustable, with a digital force readout.

Chris Goul

Staff Mechanical Design Engineer, 1X Technologies

I design mechanical hardware for production. At 1X I'm the mechanical lead for the NEO humanoid's hip and spine — three high-torque quasi-direct-drive joints — and I lead architecture for the next-generation robot. Before that I spent five years at Tesla on Megapack enclosure structures and Supercharger hardware.

Outside work I build things to find out whether the idea holds up: a cubic press that reaches multi-GPa pressures for diamond synthesis, a pneumatic home gym, an electric motorcycle. Lately I've been training language models from scratch on a single consumer GPU.

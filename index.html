01 — Materials

Synthetic Marble

A high-pressure, high-temperature press that produces 100% pure synthetic marble with no binders — replicating the geologic conditions that metamorphose limestone, so the result has the translucency that binder-based synthetics can't fake.

02 — Product

XBeams T-Slot Extrusion

A business selling lightweight, rigid plastic extrusions — a cheaper alternative to aluminium framing for robots, enclosures and shelving.

03 — Machine

Pneumatic Weight Machine

Three generations of a compact home gym that uses pneumatic actuators to apply constant, adjustable force to a cable — with a digital force readout.
